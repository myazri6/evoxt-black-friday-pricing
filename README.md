## Discounts that exist outside Black Friday

These are the offers that show up consistently across sources and don't depend on a sale date.

- **Annual billing, around 15% off.** Repeated by four aggregators in near-identical wording and described as recurring rather than first-term-only. Verify at checkout.
- **First order or first month, up to 40% off.** Also repeated across four sites, and one reviewer describes getting exactly 40% off the first month on a Windows VPS. Treat the percentage as a ceiling rather than a guarantee.
- **A small signup credit.** Community posts mention roughly $5 of credit on a new account; aggregators describe it as anywhere from 30% to 35% of the first order. The amounts don't agree, so treat it as "something small may be there" and check your balance after registering.
- **One coupon per order.** This one is consistent across a coupon site's FAQ and two separate comment threads, including a user who tried to combine a signup bonus with a Black Friday deal and was blocked. Plan on picking your single best discount rather than stacking.

## Who should not buy Evoxt

Evoxt sells unmanaged VPS. You get the server and the control panel; the web stack, the firewall rules and the application configuration are yours. One reviewer put it plainly: setup was quick, but "I did have to do all the software setup myself, as it's unmanaged, which is fine, but new users should know that."

If you want someone else to manage the server, this is the wrong product, and no discount fixes that. The same applies if you need a guided migration: one reviewer notes that Evoxt doesn't offer free migration, while DigitalOcean does. Private networking is another rough edge, with two reviewers saying the documentation wasn't clear enough and they had to open a ticket. Support answered in about three hours in one case and within an hour in another, based on a small set of reviews rather than a measured average.

Reviews across four aggregator sites run 4.3 to 4.5 with only three to six reviews each, so treat them as individual experiences rather than a consensus. What they consistently describe: deployment in minutes (fifteen for one Ubuntu box, under two for an NVMe plan), stable uptime over months of use, and prices that compare well against DigitalOcean for equivalent specs while offering less in the way of managed tooling.
